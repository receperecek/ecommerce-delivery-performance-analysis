# Project charter

## Objective
Identify where delivery delays are observed, how they relate to customer experience, and where gross order value is exposed to late delivery.

## Decision
Prioritize sellers, categories, customer states, and order-process stages for operational attention.

## Audience
Marketplace Operations Director, Seller Performance, Customer Experience, Logistics, and BI teams.

## Guardrails
Results are historical and observational. Seller involvement does not establish seller causality; reviews can be selectively missing; and at-risk order value is exposure, not confirmed loss.
